# Lollipop for iPhone

Lollipop turns real things into something you can tap. Tap a Lollipop product with your iPhone to claim it, earn points, and build your collection.

<figure><img src="../assets/ios-app/navigate/home-overview.png" alt="Lollipop home screen" width="300"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## What you can do

* **Collect**: tap Lollipop products to add them to your collection.
* **Earn points**: get Lollipop points when you claim eligible items.
* **Shop**: buy merch, drops, cards, and tags.
* **Make**: turn your own merch and playlists into things fans can tap.
* **Discover**: find brands, creators, and mixtapes.
* **Share**: get your own Lollipop Link, like `lollipop.gg/yourname`.
